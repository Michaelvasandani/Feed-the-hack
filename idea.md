# Idea

## Problem

Agencies hire UGC creators to post organic content, then have no single view of how the campaign is doing across Instagram and TikTok.

## Solution

You add the brand, brief, and creator list; we pull every matching video, including ones that show or say the product without tagging you.

One board shows campaign totals, who posted vs who went quiet, and who punched above their follower count.

We also read what each video shows and says, bucket it by format (unboxing, GRWM, review, and so on), and show which format actually won, so you know who to nudge, who to rehire, and what to brief next.
